About Our Institution

Fostering Academic Excellence in Tripura Since 2003

Managed by Private Unaided administration, Gisela English Medium High School has been a pillar of educational growth in the Silachari block of Gomati district. We provide a structured curriculum spanning Grades 1 to 10 in English medium.

Our academic year begins in April, providing students with safe classrooms, dedicated teachers, clean drinking water, and sports facilities.

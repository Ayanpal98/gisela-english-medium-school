About Our Institution

Dedicated to Educational Excellence in Gomati

Founded in 2003, Gisela English Medium High School has served as a cornerstone of learning in Silachari, fostering academic discipline, moral integrity, and modern competence.

English Medium Curriculum

Rigorous academic framework following Tripura Board standards with English as the primary instruction medium, preparing students for higher secondary and competitive futures.

Hostel Facilities

Secure and disciplined separate residential quarters for boys and girls coming from remote areas across Tripura, offering a nurturing home-away-from-home environment.

Experienced Faculty

A dedicated team of trained teaching and non-teaching staff committed to individual student mentorship, moral guidance, and holistic skill development.

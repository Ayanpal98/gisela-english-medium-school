About the school

A school rooted in learning and values.

Gisela English Medium High School serves students in Silachari with an English-medium learning environment and a focus on academic development, discipline, participation and personal growth.

The website is being developed as a single digital home for prospective parents, students, alumni and the wider school community—making key information easier to discover and future services easier to access.
